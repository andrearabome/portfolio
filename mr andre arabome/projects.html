Dedicated job scraper - Notepad
–▢✕
Flask, Javascript Python, SQLite3, HTML and CSS
https://github.com/andrearabome/let-the-jobs-find-me

To be very honest, I made this out of frustration with the job market and the lack of data available to job seekers. I wanted to create a tool that could scrape job postings from various websites, extract relevant information, and provide insights into job market trends. This project is built using Python and Flask for the backend, with a simple frontend interface for users to interact with the data.

In this case, I will only ever recieve results from two websites, with specific locations and specific fields that I would love to apply to. 

I have found great results, and I am looking to expand this project to include more websites, more locations, and more data fields. I am also looking to implement some machine learning algorithms to provide insights into job market trends and help users make informed decisions about their job search.

I also made this app out of frustration.
I was planning my week out and I felt going from calendar to reminders to my goals app was way too much work. Everything was everywhere and felt a bit overwhelming, also made me lose interest many times.
So I made a simple task app that allows me to plan my week out in one place, with a calendar view and a list view. I can also set reminders and goals for the week, and track my progress.
Only issue is due to my mac being too old, xCode and testflight are no longer supported, so seeing exactly what it looks like on my phone is quite difficult at the moment.
New mac soon and hopefully will deploy the app to the stores!
